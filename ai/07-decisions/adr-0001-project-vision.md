
# ADR-0001: Ankhora Project Vision

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026

---

# Decision

Ankhora is designed as a sovereign, cryptographically verifiable data platform.

The system provides secure ownership, collaboration, lifecycle tracking, and trusted exchange of digital information.

Ankhora is not designed as:

- a traditional cloud storage service
- a centralized database
- a simple document management system

---

# Context

Modern organizations increasingly depend on digital information but face problems:

- loss of data sovereignty
- centralized platform dependency
- unclear ownership
- insufficient auditability
- insecure collaboration
- fragmented operational history

Traditional systems optimize convenience.

Ankhora optimizes trust.

---

# Vision

The core principle:

> Data should remain owned, protected, and verifiable throughout its lifecycle.

---

# Alternatives Considered

## Traditional SaaS Platform

Rejected.

Reason:

Creates centralized ownership and dependency.

---

## Shared Enterprise Database

Rejected.

Reason:

Does not preserve sovereignty between organizations.

---

## Blockchain-First Architecture

Rejected.

Reason:

Blockchain is used selectively for verification, not as the primary application layer.

---

# Consequences

Positive:

- strong ownership model
- enterprise trust
- multi-organization collaboration
- long-term scalability

Negative:

- higher architectural complexity
- stronger security requirements
- more explicit design decisions

---

# AI Rules

When making architectural decisions, AI must preserve:

```

User ownership

Cryptographic trust

Context separation

Long-term evolution

```

Do not introduce designs that transform Ankhora into a centralized platform.

---

# Final Principle

Ankhora exists to create trusted digital environments where organizations can collaborate without surrendering sovereignty.

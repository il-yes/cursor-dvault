# Project Vision

## Name

Ankhora

---

# Mission

Ankhora is a secure collaboration platform that enables individuals and organizations to create, protect, share, and govern sensitive information through cryptographic trust rather than centralized control.

The platform combines secure storage, collaborative workspaces, versioned business objects, federation, and compliance into a unified architecture.

---

# Vision

Create the world's most trusted platform for secure collaboration.

Trust should emerge from cryptography, transparency, verifiable history, and open architecture—not from blind reliance on service providers.

---

# Core Principles

Ankhora is built around several foundational principles.

## Security by Design

Security is never an afterthought.

Every component should assume hostile environments.

Encryption, signatures, access control, and auditing are first-class concerns.

---

## Privacy by Default

Users own their data.

Sensitive information should remain encrypted whenever possible.

The platform should minimize exposure of plaintext information.

---

## Trust through Verification

Trust is established through cryptographic verification.

Every important operation should be verifiable.

---

## Collaboration without Compromising Security

Organizations should collaborate while maintaining independent ownership of their information.

---

## Domain-Driven Design

Business domains drive architecture.

Technology never dictates domain boundaries.

---

## Modularity

Every bounded context has a single responsibility.

Modules communicate through explicit interfaces.

---

## Extensibility

Every major subsystem should support future extensions.

New business domains should integrate without architectural rewrites.

---

# Platform Components

The platform currently consists of several major systems.

- Identity
- Vault
- TraceCore
- Federation
- Realtime
- Subscription
- C3 Collaboration

Additional bounded contexts may be introduced as the platform evolves.

---

# Target Domains

Ankhora is designed as a platform rather than a vertical product.

Potential domains include:

- Healthcare
- Construction
- Manufacturing
- Banking
- Government
- Legal
- Supply Chain
- Research
- Education

Each domain reuses the same secure platform.

---

# Success Criteria

Success is measured by:

- architectural quality
- developer productivity
- system reliability
- security
- scalability
- maintainability
- user trust

Not by lines of code.

---

# Guiding Principle

Every engineering decision should answer one question:

Does this make the platform more trustworthy?
